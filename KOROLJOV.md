Koroljov holds what the boards are put into and how a unit is carried: the Korabls, the box of
each unit; the unit on the body; Stykovka, the contact in the lid; Semjorka, the case for a set.
The boards themselves are Materinka's ([`BOARD.md`](BOARD.md)), their parts in
[`PARTS.md`](PARTS.md).

## The Korabls

**A Korabl is a sealed box, glued shut**: the protection against water is the box's own, its
openings for the light sealed; its one contact, Stykovka's, glued into the lid
([`PARTS.md`](PARTS.md), *The inlet*); the unit worked through the box by the magnets' Hall
switches ([`PARTS.md`](PARTS.md), *The magnets' switches*). **Two are drawn**: the Belka's, which
Zvjozdočka and Ugoljok share, Ugoljok's built sturdier; and Kryska's, of its own size, built sturdy
as standard ([`KRYSKA.md`](KRYSKA.md)). Each is drawn after its board.

**The Belka's box is chosen with the drawn board.** The 1551G's inside is 44,73 × 29,73 × 16,00 mm
(Hammond's drawing), narrower than the chosen cell's 30 mm and lower than the Belka's stack
([`PARTS.md`](PARTS.md), *The cells*).

**The Belka's cells that may be fitted**, all LTOPOW's LiFePO4 pouches from the same list, each with
its protection board and the same charger settings; the hours scale with the capacity. The box is
chosen with the drawn board: inside, the 1551H is 54,6 × 29,7 × 16,0 mm and the 1551K 74,73 ×
34,73 × 16,00 (Hammond's drawings), so that of the 1551 only the 1551K takes a cell 30 mm wide;

## The unit on the body — what research has used

How a unit is held decides more of the measure than its sensor does ([`PRINCIPLE.md`](PRINCIPLE.md),
*The sensor's noise against the motion*). No study found compares straps, glue and boots on one
animal; what each used, and saw:

- **The hoof**: hot glue over the dorsal wall at the gallop ([Parkes et al.,
  2020](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244105)); hot glue and stiff cloth tape on the lateral
  wall ([Bruce et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12917-026-05376-0)); a 70 g logger in an
  aluminium bracket glued by hoof adhesive on every hoof, a run made again whenever one fell off
  ([Horan et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.3390/ani12172161), 2.3, 2.4); fixing a unit on the hoof
  slow and a risk to the sensor ([Briggs and Mazzà,
  2021](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0254813)).
- **The pastern and the cannon**: for the hoof's contact the pastern better than the cannon, 1–6 ms
  from the hoof's own events, in Velcro wraps and tape, the cannon in commercial pockets
  (Briggs and Mazzà, 2021); a custom holster on the lateral cannon ([Bosch et al.,
  2018](https://doi.org/10.3390/s18030850)); double-sided tape and vet-wrap on the medial cannon
  (Bruce et al., 2026); sites over little soft tissue, to keep the artefact down ([Greco-Otto
  et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12917-019-2075-6)).
- **The trunk**: on cows, cases glued by cyanoacrylate and taken off with acetone, the limbs on
  straps at the mid-metacarpus and metatarsus ([Leclercq et al.,
  2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.animal.2024.101269)); a collar's unit came loose twice ([Tijssen
  et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253479)); a unit on the tubera sacrale rolls
  over the spinous processes, so the pelvis's roll was taken from the tubera coxae (Leclercq et al.,
  2024). On a standing horse's pelvis, sensors on the skin read the bone's flexion smaller than
  sensors on pins in it, 0,98° against 1,61° on average, and agreed "mostly weak" in it ([Goff
  et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jevs.2018.01.007), table 5).
- **A dog**: above the carpus and the tarsus, a unit below the carpus having "obstructed walking"
  ([Jenkins et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0198893)); a 12 g IMU in a 19 g
  3D-printed holder with a cushion pad and Velcro ([Zhang et al.,
  2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-08676-1)); on a collar, a second unit on it, a leash, a
  case or a loose tie lowered two monitors' agreement from r 0,92–0,93 to 0,43–0,79 ([Martin et al.,
  2017](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12917-017-0971-1), table 1); a harness changed no stride's time
  ([Duerr et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.3415/VCOT-16-01-0012), its abstract).
- **A cow's leg**: at the metatarsus the claw's events in time, its peaks lower than at the claw
  ([Alsaaod et al., 2017](https://doi.org/10.3168/jds.2016-11678), its abstract).
- **A pig**: adhesive squares on the shoulders and the neck (the Czech utility model CZ 34125 U1).
- **The mass**: 20 g the ProMove-mini (Bosch et al., 2018); 20 g a dog's own unit of 20 × 35 × 35 mm
  (Jenkins et al., 2018); 31 g the IMU and its holder (Zhang et al., 2022); 70 g the hoof's logger
  (Horan et al., 2022).
- **The time**: taps on the units, a clapper board or a shaken limb, aligned afterwards (Parkes
  et al., 2020; Zhang et al., 2022; Jenkins et al., 2018; Tijssen et al., 2021), none giving the
  accuracy left; EquiMoves states its units within 100 ns, its method not given (Bosch et al.,
  2018).

## Stykovka and Semjorka

**Stykovka** is the magnetic contact, its parts and its wiring in [`PARTS.md`](PARTS.md), *The
inlet*: on a lead, or on a station for the whole set with a USB hub. **Semjorka** is the case that
carries a whole set.
